## My engineering interview method

1. **Problem:** What failed or needed to change, for whom, and what are the constraints?
2. **Investigation:** What observable evidence identifies the cause, and what remains uncertain?
3. **Change:** What is the smallest reviewable fix with an explicit acceptance criterion?
4. **Verification:** Which exact source version, test command, output and runtime were checked?
5. **Limitations:** What is untested, environment-dependent, awaiting approval or still incomplete?

I use AI-assisted coding when appropriate and take responsibility for direction, review, testing and clearly communicating limitations. I do not claim to have manually written every line.
