## Engineering approach

My work emphasizes clear requirements, version-specific verification, observable failure handling, security boundaries and accountable use of AI coding tools.

- **Specifications and implementation:** describe the behavior, identify ownership and interfaces, and keep changes small enough to review.
- **Testing and reliability:** use reproducible checks and actual browser or runtime evidence where available. Separate local results from hosted CI.
- **Operations and safety:** protect credentials and customer data, limit destructive actions, and preserve independent approval or merge checks.
- **Communication:** state project status, what was verified, and what remains incomplete.

I coordinate product and engineering direction and use AI-assisted implementation when it is appropriate. I do not claim personally to have authored every line of code in these repositories.
